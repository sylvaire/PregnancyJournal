# MIUI app notification settings (opened via the app's 系统通知设置 button)

Activity: com.android.settings/.Settings$AppNotificationSettingsActivity

Rows and read-back switch state:
- 允许通知 ... checked=true
- 焦点通知 (将关键通知以特殊样式展示) ... checked=true
- 通知权限设置
- 桌面角标 ... checked=false
- 悬浮通知 (允许在屏幕上方弹出通知) ... checked=true
- 锁屏通知 (允许在锁屏时进行通知提醒) ... checked=true
- 发声 ... checked=true
- 振动 ... checked=true
- 常驻通知 ... checked=true
- 通知类别: 孕迹提醒 - 产检、用药和自定义本地提醒；锁屏默认隐藏具体内容

Returning with BACK goes back to the app (the system settings activity is opened inside the app task).
